FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

How do I create an account on WeaveHub?

Download the WeaveHub app on iOS or Android, open it, and tap "Sign Up". You can register with your email address, or use Google or Apple Sign-In for a faster setup.

How do I become a vendor on WeaveHub?

After creating a regular account, navigate to the "Become a Vendor" section in the app settings or profile screen. Fill in your store details (name, logo, banner) and start listing your products.

How do I track my order?

Go to "My Orders" in the app. Tap on any order to see its current status, estimated delivery date, and shipping details. You'll also receive push notifications for status updates.

How does the in-app wallet work?

Your WeaveHub Wallet lets you top up a balance that can be used to pay for orders quickly. You can view transaction history, pending balances, and top up via supported payment methods.

What are live shopping streams?

Vendors can go live within the app to showcase their products in real-time. As a buyer, you can watch the stream, ask questions in the live chat, and purchase products directly during the stream.

How do I request a refund?

Navigate to "My Orders", select the order in question, and tap "Request Refund". You can also contact the vendor directly through in-app chat or reach out to our support team at hello@weavehub.io.

How do I delete my account?

You can request account deletion from the app Settings screen under "Account". Alternatively, email us at privacy@weavehub.io with your request and we'll process it within 7 business days.

Is WeaveHub available outside Nigeria?

WeaveHub is currently optimised for Nigeria and West Africa but is available for download worldwide. We're actively expanding to more regions — stay tuned for updates!
